# Power BI Portfolio

This repository showcases Power BI analytics projects focused on transforming data into actionable business insights.  
Each project addresses real-world business questions using structured data modeling, clear KPIs, and decision-oriented dashboards.

---

## 🔍 Core Focus Areas
- Business performance monitoring  
- Sales and profitability analysis  
- Decision support dashboards  
- Executive and operational reporting  

---

## 📊 Projects

### 1️⃣ Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Analytics
**Domain:** Pharmaceutical Distribution  

Analytics project developed in response to a *Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Analytics Data Challenge*, focused on evaluating commercial performance across countries, regions, and pharmacies.

**Key aspects:**
- Revenue, margin, and volume analysis over time  
- Geographic performance comparison (country → region → pharmacy)  
- Product category and brand profitability  
- Impact of promotions on sales and margin  
- Identification of high-volume vs high-margin products  

📁 Project folder: `Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Analytics`

---

### 2️⃣ Athlete Health & Injury Analytics
**Domain:** Sports & Performance Analytics  

Project focused on monitoring athlete health indicators and injury patterns to support preventive decision-making and performance optimization.

**Key aspects:**
- Injury frequency and severity analysis  
- Player availability tracking  
- Temporal trends in injuries  
- Support for medical and technical staff decisions  

📁 Project folder: `Athlete Health & Injury Analytics`
